Six signs of slop

Pick one to see it declanked.

Instagram caption · Dry cleaner

SLOP“Elevate your wardrobe with our premium garment care solutions!”

DECLANKED“Wine on the white shirt? Bring it in before it sets. We’ve saved worse.”

Extra fingers, melted signs and food you’ve never served.

Menu photo · Restaurant

SLOPAn AI-made plate of enchiladas, with a fork melting into the salsa.

DECLANKEDA phone photo of the real plate, taken by the front window during the lunch rush.

Every customer gets “valuable feedback,” whatever they actually said.

Review reply · Dental office

SLOP“Thank you for your valuable feedback. We strive to provide exceptional care.”

DECLANKED“Thanks, Ruben. Glad the crown finally feels like your own tooth. See you in six months.”

Plenty of posts, but nothing only your business could say.

Blog post · Pest control

SLOP“The Ultimate Guide to Pest Control: Everything You Need to Know”

DECLANKED“Scorpion season in Tucson: where they hide in your house, and what actually keeps them out.”

Your Tuesday lunch special probably doesn’t need a manifesto.

Specials post · Restaurant

SLOP“Indulge in a symphony of flavors with our exquisite new seasonal menu.”

DECLANKED“Hatch chiles are in. The green chile burger is back through October, or until we run out.”

A chatbot shouldn’t invent your prices or your policies.

Chatbot answer · HVAC

SLOP“Absolutely! All estimates are free, and we guarantee the lowest price in Arizona.”

DECLANKED“Estimates are free inside Tucson city limits. Farther out there’s a $49 trip fee, credited if you book the job.”

BEFORE YOU ASK

A few things
off the counter

We’re not on social media. Can you still help?

Yes. Content is where a lot of businesses start, but plenty of the work is behind the counter: answering inquiries, following up on quotes, keeping notes and schedules straight. Ask for an AI Checkup instead of a Slop Check.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any small business with repetitive work. Dry cleaners, dental offices, restaurants, home services. If part of your day is the same task over and over, there’s usually something worth setting up.

Is all AI content slop?

No. We use AI ourselves; the problem is generic, inaccurate or unreviewed work reaching your customers.

Can you tell if something was written by AI?

We don't promise to identify its author. We look at how it reads: whether it's accurate, specific, useful and recognizably your business.

Can you build our first website?

Yes. A one- to three-page site starts at $900. We’ll agree on the pages, business details, and contact options before we start.

What happens after handoff?

You keep control of your accounts. Every build comes with written instructions and a 30-day window for fixes to the agreed setup. You can book a one-time Tune-Up from $150 for a later review or refresh.

Why “declank”?

“Clanker” means robot. We take the robot out of your writing. The repair shop seemed less depressing than another consultancy acronym.

Do you only work in Tucson?

Tucson first, but happy to work with customers remotely from anywhere.
