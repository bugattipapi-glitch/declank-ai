BEFORE YOU ASK

A few things
off the counter

Is all AI content slop?

No. We use AI ourselves; the problem is generic, inaccurate or unreviewed work reaching your customers.

Can you tell if something was written by AI?

We don't promise to identify its author. We look at how it reads: whether it's accurate, specific, useful and recognizably your business.

Can you help with a broader AI strategy?

Yes. Slop cleanup is the starting point, not the whole shop. We can help you choose tools, train your team and find sensible opportunities to automate work.

Do you write all our posts every month?

The focus is fixing what's there and helping your team keep it good. This isn't a monthly content mill.

Why “declank”?

“Clanker” means robot. We take the robot out of your writing. The repair shop seemed less depressing than another consultancy acronym.

Do you only work in Tucson?

Tucson first. Especially local hospitality, tourism and home services businesses that want someone who can sit down with the team.
