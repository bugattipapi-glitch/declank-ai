BEFORE YOU ASK

A few things
off the counter

We’re not on social media. Can you still help?

Yes. Content is where a lot of businesses start, but plenty of the work is behind the counter: answering inquiries, following up on quotes, keeping notes and schedules straight. Ask for an AI Checkup instead of a Slop Check.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any small business with repetitive work. Dry cleaners, dental offices, restaurants, home services. If part of your day is the same task over and over, there’s usually something worth setting up.

Is all AI content slop?

No. We use AI ourselves; the problem is generic, inaccurate or unreviewed work reaching your customers.

Can you tell if something was written by AI?

We don't promise to identify its author. We look at how it reads: whether it's accurate, specific, useful and recognizably your business.

Do you write all our posts every month?

The focus is fixing what's there and helping your team keep it good. This isn't a monthly content mill.

Why “declank”?

“Clanker” means robot. We take the robot out of your writing. The repair shop seemed less depressing than another consultancy acronym.

Do you only work in Tucson?

Tucson first, but happy to work with customers remotely from anywhere.
